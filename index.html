Bathtub Reglazing FAQs

What is bathtub reglazing?

Bathtub reglazing, also called refinishing or resurfacing, is the process of repairing a tub's surface and applying a new durable coating. It removes the look of stains, dullness, and minor damage, and gives the tub a smooth, glossy finish without replacing it.

Is reglazing cheaper than replacing my bathtub?

In most cases, yes. Replacement involves removing the old tub, plumbing work, and often repairing surrounding walls and tile. Reglazing avoids that demolition and is usually much more affordable, as well as faster.

How long does the job take?

Most tub reglazing projects are completed in a day, followed by a curing period before the tub is used. We will confirm the exact timeline and any care instructions when you book, since it depends on the size and condition of the surface.

Can you reglaze tile as well as bathtubs?

Yes. Our tile reglazing service covers wall and floor tile in bathrooms, and we can refinish your tub and tile together so they match. Call us for guidance on your specific surfaces.

How do I care for my tub after reglazing?

Use non-abrasive cleaners and a soft cloth or sponge, and avoid harsh scrubbing pads. We will give you simple care tips after the job so your finish stays glossy for as long as possible.
